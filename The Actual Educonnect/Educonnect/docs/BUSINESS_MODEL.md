# EduConnect — Simple Business Model

A one-page business model for EduConnect (AI-enabled peer-to-peer learning platform). All figures are in **Ugandan Shillings (UGX)**.

---

## 1. Customer Segments

| Segment | Description | Size / focus |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| **University students (primary)** | Undergraduates and postgraduates at Ugandan universities (e.g. Makerere, UCU, Kyambogo, Mbarara, Busitema, Gulu) across all fields: Computing, Law, Business, Education, Humanities, Health, Agriculture. | Main user base; sign up for study partners, quizzes, resources. |
| **Institutions (B2B)** | Universities, colleges, or departments that want a branded or licensed learning-and-matching tool for their students. | Fewer, high-value accounts; licence or subscription per institution. |
| **Secondary / future** | Secondary school students (e.g. A-Level) or lifelong learners seeking structured study and peer support. | Expansion segment. |

---

## 2. Value Proposition

**For students**

- **Find the right study partners** — Matching by degree, course area, interests, and study style so students can form effective study groups.
- **One place for learning and progress** — Curated resources by field, quizzes, EduBot support, and a personalised study plan (including ML-based “next topic” suggestions).
- **Inclusive and multi-field** — Supports many programmes (not only computing), so law, business, education, health, agriculture, and humanities students get relevant content and peers.
- **Low friction** — Free or low-cost access; works on typical devices and networks in Uganda.

**For institutions**

- **Better engagement and outcomes** — Students are more engaged and better connected, with visibility (e.g. through admin dashboards) into usage and activity.
- **Local and relevant** — Built around Ugandan curricula and universities (MAK, UCU, etc.) and priced in UGX.

---

## 3. Revenue Model (UGX)

| Stream | Description | Example pricing (UGX) |
|--------|-------------|------------------------|
| **Freemium — students** | Free: sign up, basic matching, limited resources and quizzes. Premium: unlimited resources, priority matching, export progress, ad-free. | **Free**; Premium **15,000 – 25,000 UGX per semester** (or 40,000 – 60,000 UGX per year). |
| **Institution licence (B2B)** | Per-university or per-department licence: branded access, admin dashboard, usage reports, optional SSO. | **2,000,000 – 8,000,000 UGX per institution per year** (tiered by enrolled students). |
| **Sponsored content / partnerships** | Education publishers, exam prep, or EdTech partners pay for featured resources or placement (clearly labelled). | **500,000 – 2,000,000 UGX per campaign** (one-off or per semester). |
| **Optional: certification / badges** | Paid micro-credentials or completion certificates for quiz tracks or learning paths. | **5,000 – 15,000 UGX per certificate**. |

**Illustrative annual revenue (early stage)**

- 500 paying students × 20,000 UGX/semester × 2 ≈ **20,000,000 UGX**
- 2 institutions × 4,000,000 UGX ≈ **8,000,000 UGX**
- 2 sponsored campaigns × 1,000,000 UGX ≈ **2,000,000 UGX**  
- **Total ≈ 30,000,000 UGX per year** (before scaling).

## 4. Cost Structure (UGX)

| Category | Item | Monthly (UGX) | Annual (UGX) |
|----------|------|----------------|--------------|
| **Technology** | Hosting (e.g. VPS / small cloud), domain, SSL | 400,000 – 800,000 | 4,800,000 – 9,600,000 |
| **Technology** | APIs (e.g. email, SMS, optional AI/ML services) | 200,000 – 500,000 | 2,400,000 – 6,000,000 |
| **People** | Part-time dev / maintenance (1 person) | 1,500,000 – 2,500,000 | 18,000,000 – 30,000,000 |
| **People** | Part-time support / community (1 person) | 800,000 – 1,200,000 | 9,600,000 – 14,400,000 |
| **Marketing** | Social media, campus activations, referrals | 300,000 – 600,000 | 3,600,000 – 7,200,000 |
| **Legal / admin** | Registration, contracts, basic compliance | 100,000 – 200,000 | 1,200,000 – 2,400,000 |
| **Contingency** | Buffer (e.g. 10–15%) | 400,000 – 600,000 | 4,800,000 – 7,200,000 |
| **Total** | | **3,700,000 – 6,400,000** | **44,400,000 – 76,800,000** |

**Break-even (simplified)**

- If **costs ≈ 50,000,000 UGX/year** and **revenue ≈ 30,000,000 UGX/year** in year one, the gap is covered by grants, seed funding, or cost cuts (e.g. lower hosting, volunteer support).
- Target: **break even in year 2–3** as paying students and institutions grow (e.g. 2,000+ paying students and 5–10 institutions).
